About me

Healthcare shaped how I engineer.

Before software, I worked as a physiotherapist and completed a master’s degree in Rehabilitation Sciences. That background taught me to observe barriers closely, listen before proposing solutions, and understand that “works” is not the same as “works for everyone.”

Today I develop Flutter products with distributed teams and collaborate directly with U.S.-based clients in English—from requirements and technical decisions through builds and releases.
